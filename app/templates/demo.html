Live demo: report an animal in 3 taps

This is the real app talking to the real AI. Pick a scenario, or type and send your own photo. The left phone is the citizen. The right phone shows the responder who gets the alert, in their own language.

  1. 1Tap “Hurt animal: SOS”

    No sign-up, no app store. Works in any phone browser, in 6 languages.

  2. 2Send a photo, or just words

    “kutta ghayal hai” is enough. At night, words alone work.

  3. 3Share your location

    You get a case number and a link to track every step until the animal is safe.
