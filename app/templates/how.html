How it works

One StrayWatch AI with six agents in one room. The AI reads and compares; plain, tested code decides urgency and who goes. Every step is written to a case ledger anyone can replay.

The six steps

with a real example
  1. 1Spotted

    A citizen sends a photo + location, or a road camera sees an animal in a lane too long.

    example: “kutta ghayal hai, pair se khoon”
  2. 2Read

    Triage agent: species, injury, on the road, blocking a lane. The reporter's words override a calm-looking photo.

    target < 5 s · measured ~2–3 s
  3. 3Known animal?

    Identify agent compares registered animals within 1 km. Has a caretaker? They hear first.

    target < 3 s · measured ~1.2 s
  4. 4Routed

    Rules set urgency and pick the nearest right team.

    alert < 30 s
  5. 5Escalated

    No accept? Watch agent asks the next one, then the duty coordinator. The reporter is kept updated.

    3 min (HIGH) / 10 min (CHECK) per hop
  6. 6Closed + learned

    Closed only with a photo, checked by AI. Hotspot map and health diary updated.

    proof required

Urgency rules

code, not AI
LevelWhenWho is asked
HIGH · URGENTLane blocked on a main road or at peak hour (8–11 AM, 5–8 PM) · aggressive animal near traffic · hurt or trapped animal on the road · the person on the spot says it's hurtCaretaker → NGO / vet, municipal cattle team, ABC team or traffic police (by case) → volunteers → duty coordinator. 3 minutes each.
CHECKInjured, sick, limping or lying still off the road · blockage on a minor road off-peak · dead animalSame order, 10 minutes each.
LOWOff the road and not hurtNobody is disturbed. Logged for the hotspot map. The citizen can tap “It's hurt” any time.

Same input → same urgency, every time: 12 unit tests in tests/test_rules.py.

One AI, six agents

why not one big model?
AI · vision LLM

Triage agent

Reads photo + text in 6 languages into a strict, validated record. Asks if the location is missing.

AI · vision LLM

Identify agent

Matches a photo to nearby registered animals and lost posters. Acts alone only at 95%+; otherwise asks a human.

AI · YOLOv8 at the edge

Camera agent

Flags an animal standing or lying still in a lane past a time limit, on hotspot cameras.

Rules + map

Dispatch agent

Applies the rules and distance, alerts the right responder, checks the proof photo.

Timers

Watch agent

Times every open case; escalates to the next responder, then the coordinator.

Data queries

Hotspot agent

Turns repeat cases into actions: “cattle team to Ratu Road at 5 PM”.

Why split it?
  • Safe: an LLM never sets urgency or picks the team; unit-tested code does.
  • Testable: each agent has its own test. Swappable: change a model without touching dispatch.
  • Accountable: every hand-off is in one ledger. See it live in the Agent Room →
