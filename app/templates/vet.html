How a consult works

  1. 1Tell us

    Pet or street animal, which animal, a photo and a few words in any of 6 languages.

  2. 2Safe first steps

    A vet-reviewed care card and an urgency. Emergencies go to a clinic or 1962 first.

  3. 3A vet joins

    Pick a vet online. They see a one-page summary before they say yes.

    next vet after 5 min
  4. 4Chat or video

    Each side reads in their own language. Photos and a video link.

  5. 5Vet's note + follow-up

    The vet's advice is saved in the animal's record. We check in after 2 days.

The AI organises and translates; only the vet advises. StrayWatch's AI never diagnoses, names a medicine or gives a dose.

Please don't

common remedies that harm
✗
Kerosene, engine oil or phenyl on wounds

They burn the skin and can poison the animal. A wound with maggots needs a vet the same day.

✗
Human medicines

Many common painkillers are poisonous to dogs and cats. Only give what a vet prescribes.

✗
Food in plastic bags for cows

Cows swallow the plastic with the food, and it builds up in their stomach.
