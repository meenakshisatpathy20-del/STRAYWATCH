Camera AI

A small box next to a hotspot camera runs YOLOv8 at 1–2 frames a second. When a cow or dog stands or lies in a traffic lane past a time limit, it sends one event to the Agent Room. From there it's a normal case: urgency rules, the right team, timed escalation, proof photo. Cameras are optional: citizen reports work from day one.

  1. 1Detect + track

    YOLOv8n finds cows and dogs; ByteTrack follows each one across frames.

    ~35 ms / frame on a 4-core CPU
  2. 2Lane check

    Is the animal's position inside this camera's lane polygon?

  3. 3Two timers

    Dwell: in the lane too long (60 s in the pilot). Still: hardly moved for 30 s.

  4. 4One event

    Snapshot + facts posted to the Agent Room, once per animal.

    REST agent protocol
  5. 5Case + signboard

    Rules make a blocked main road HIGH; the cattle team is alerted; signboards warn drivers.
