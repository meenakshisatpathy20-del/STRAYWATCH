Camera AI

A small box next to a hotspot camera runs YOLOv8 at 1–2 frames a second. It looks for one thing: an animal lying in a traffic lane and not moving, which is likely hurt or hit, and an accident waiting to happen. Healthy animals standing, eating or walking are ignored. Cameras are optional: citizen reports work from day one.

  1. 1Detect + track

    YOLOv8n finds cows and dogs; ByteTrack follows each one across frames.

    ~35 ms / frame on a 4-core CPU
  2. 2Lane check

    Is the animal's position inside this camera's lane polygon?

  3. 3Lying + not moving

    Body low and wide, or it went down from standing, and hardly moved for 30 s.

  4. 4One event

    Snapshot + facts posted to the Agent Room, once per animal.

    REST agent protocol
  5. 5AI checks, rules decide

    The Triage agent reads the snapshot. Hurt → URGENT: rescue NGO, vet, police to guard the spot, signboards on. Healthy and resting → only logged.
