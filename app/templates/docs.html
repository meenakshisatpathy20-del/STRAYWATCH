Limitations

what we're careful not to claim
  • Responder teams on this site are samples. Real NGOs, vets and municipal teams join in the pilot.
  • Camera AI uses a pretrained model: cows are reliable, street dogs are not yet (3/7). Fine-tuning on Indian road footage and night video is next.
  • Re-identification was tested on edited copies of the same photos, which is easier than real life.
  • The free server sleeps when idle; the first request can take up to a minute.
  • The AI can misread a photo. That's why rules decide urgency, the reporter's words win, and people confirm matches.
