What StrayWatch does

detect · respond · prevent
👁️

Detect

  • Citizens: a photo or just words, by app or WhatsApp, in 6 languages
  • Road cameras: AI flags an animal standing or lying in a lane too long
  • Caretakers: daily check-ins on the animals they feed
How camera AI works →
🚨

Respond

  • AI reads the case; tested rules set urgency (HIGH / CHECK / LOW)
  • Caretaker first, then the right team: NGO, vet, municipal cattle team, ABC team, police
  • No reply? It moves on by itself, then the duty coordinator. Closed only with a photo.

Prevent

  • Hotspots by road and hour, so teams act before the rush
  • Signboard warnings for drivers, no app needed
