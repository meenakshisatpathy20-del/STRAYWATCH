The gap

help exists, it just arrives late

Every day, stray animals are hit, injured or fall sick on our roads, and help reaches them late. People don't know whom to call; rescuers and vets find out without a photo or exact location; nobody owns the case. And an injured animal lying on the road becomes the next accident.

What StrayWatch does

safety · treatment · fewer accidents
🚨

Rescue

  • A photo or just words, in 6 languages; road cameras spot an animal lying in a lane
  • AI reads it; tested rules set urgency. Hurt on the road = URGENT
  • Caretaker first, then rescue NGO and vet; police guard the spot on busy roads. Closed only with a photo
Watch the Agent Room →
🩺

Treatment

  • Online vets for strays and pets, verified
  • AI writes the case summary for the vet; emergencies go to 1962
  • Health record, vaccine reminders and food advice
Open Vet care →
🛣️

Fewer accidents

  • Signboards warn drivers while an injured animal is on the road
  • Hotspots show where animals keep getting hurt, so the city can add lights or speed breakers
  • Lost & Found brings lost animals home
Open the city dashboard →

Healthy animals standing, eating or wandering are not cases: nobody is sent, and StrayWatch never removes healthy strays.
