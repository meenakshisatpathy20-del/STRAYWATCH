Three things StrayWatch does

all inside WhatsApp
{{ d.heart(32) }}

My Animals

For the feeders and caretakers who look after street dogs and cows every day.

  • One photo makes a profile: colour, markings, ear notch
  • Daily photo: it recognises who it is and keeps a health diary
  • Spots changes like a new wound, and reminds you about rabies shots

Rescue SOS

See a hurt animal? Send a photo and your location.

  • AI checks how urgent it is, even from a Hinglish message
  • If it's a known animal, its caretaker hears first
  • No reply in 3 minutes? It asks the next helper, by itself

Lost & Found

Lost your cow or dog? Post a photo.

  • It searches every photo people sent nearby
  • Checks each new photo too, and pings you on a match
  • A shareable poster, without your number or exact spot

Our promise to animals and people

{{ d.paw(22) }}
The AI chooses, it never invents first aid

Safety tips come only from reviewed cards based on People For Animals, Humane World for Animals, Blue Cross and WHO, with sources shown.

{{ d.paw(22) }}
Private by default

No phone numbers are shared. Public posters show only an area. Photos are stripped of hidden location data.

{{ d.paw(22) }}
For animals, never against them

There is no "remove this dog" button. StrayWatch follows India's Animal Birth Control Rules: care, vaccinate, sterilise.

{{ d.paw(22) }}
Humans confirm, AI assists

When it's unsure who an animal is, it asks. It only acts on its own when it's very sure (90%+).

{{ d.paw(22) }}
Helpers are protected too

Alerts only within {{ 8 }} km, at most 6 a day, and "pause" any time. Fake photos with no animal are stopped.

{{ d.paw(22) }}
Not an emergency service

StrayWatch helps people help animals. If a person is hurt, call 112.
